# Vowels — emotional gestures + picture-stories

Vowels use a **feeling/gesture** first, then a short **picture-story** that carries that mood. Print art hides the capital letter in the scene.

## Locked map

| Letter | Sound | Gesture | Picture-story | Letter-in-picture idea |
|--------|--------|---------|---------------|------------------------|
| **A** | Ah | Surprise, awe, admiration | Grimm *The Star Money* (stars fall; wonder) | A as peaks of light / star-rays |
| **E** | Ay / Eh | Separation, boundary | Aesop *The Two Goats* (narrow bridge) | E as bridge post + three rails |
| **I** | Ee | Upright “I am” | Andersen *Steadfast Tin Soldier* (soft) | I as soldier / tall fir |
| **O** | Oh | Embrace, wholeness | European *Stone Soup* (village circle) | O as pot / moon / circle |
| **U** | Oo | Hold close, mystery | Andersen *Thumbelina* opening (flower cup) | U as cupped petals / hands |

## Optional frame

Michael Seifert, *The Land of the Singing Sounds* — tell from your copy if you use it. Not reprinted here.

## Classroom use

1. Gesture + singing sound  
2. Tell the short picture-story  
3. Show/print the page; children find the letter in the picture  
4. Form drawing from the same feeling
